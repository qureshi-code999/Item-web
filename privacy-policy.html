1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the ZS Mart mobile application and website (operated by ZS Mart, located at Allah Wala Town Sector H, Korangi, Karachi, Pakistan). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparent information practices.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly provided by you during your shopping and ordering experience:

  • Contact Information: Your name and mobile/WhatsApp telephone number provided during order creation to confirm and coordinate delivery.
  • Delivery Address: Street address or area details you enter so our riders can deliver your grocery order to your doorstep.
  • Cart & Favorites: Saved locally on your device (using secure localStorage) for your convenience so your items remain when you reopen the app.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used (Play Store Data Safety)

The ZS Mart application requests the following Android device permissions solely for user-initiated in-app functionality:

  • Voice Product Search (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively when you tap the microphone button in the search bar to perform a voice search for items. Audio is converted to text instantly via on-device/OS speech recognizer. We do NOT record, stream, save, or store your voice audio on any remote server.
  • Delivery Location (android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used exclusively to auto-fill your delivery address when placing an order. Your coordinates are converted to an address string on your device and are never tracked in the background or shared with third parties.
  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to fetch live catalog prices, product images, and transmit your order details to WhatsApp.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Prepare and format your grocery order bill for WhatsApp checkout.
  • Contact you regarding order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery updates.
  • Provide local customer support for exchanges or inquiries.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties. Order messages are routed directly to our official store WhatsApp business line (+92 336 8945775) upon your confirmation.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your contact records at any time. Simply send a message to our store on WhatsApp or call +92 336 8945775, and we will promptly remove your records from our dispatch system.
